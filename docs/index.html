The honest blocker

iOS 10 arm64 cannot be built from open source. Modern XNU (iOS 16 era) builds arm64 because Apple Silicon made those configs public. The iOS 10 snapshot predates that and is x86_64-only. So the clean route is closed; what remains is Apple's encrypted kernelcache (gray) or a newer iOS (wrong ABI for iOS 10 userland).
